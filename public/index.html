Why add my own guide?

If your IPTV provider gives you an XMLTV guide URL, it's always included in matching alongside the public sources. It's built for your exact channel lineup, so it usually matches far better. Put one URL per line to combine several.

What's the channel backlog?

Every channel you've manually fixed (click a tvg-id or logo to edit) can be exported as a small XMLTV file. Commit it to your own repo and add its raw URL to "Your own guide URL(s)" so future imports resolve those channels automatically.
